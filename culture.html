Local Life and Festivals

Music and Dance

Douala is widely recognized as the birthplace of Makossa, one of Cameroon's most celebrated music genres. Makossa combines traditional rhythms with jazz, funk, and modern pop influences, creating a unique sound enjoyed across Africa and beyond. Music is an essential part of everyday life in Douala and is featured at weddings, festivals, cultural celebrations, and public events. Traditional dances are performed alongside contemporary styles, reflecting the city's rich cultural heritage and vibrant spirit.

Food

Douala offers a rich and diverse culinary experience that reflects the traditions of Cameroon. Popular dishes include Ndolé, a flavorful meal made with bitter leaves, peanuts, and meat or fish; Eru, prepared with leafy vegetables and water fufu; Koki, made from ground beans wrapped in leaves; and freshly grilled fish, a local favorite often served with fried plantains or cassava. Street food such as roasted corn, grilled meat (soya), and puff-puff and pap is also widely enjoyed. Sharing meals with family and friends is an important part of Douala's culture and hospitality.

Art and Tradition

Douala is home to a thriving artistic community that celebrates both tradition and innovation. Local artisans produce beautiful wood carvings, woven baskets, beadwork, paintings, and sculptures inspired by the customs of the Sawa people and other ethnic communities. Cultural festivals, traditional ceremonies, and storytelling help preserve the city's history and values. Museums, art galleries, and public exhibitions also showcase the creativity and cultural identity that make Douala one of Cameroon's artistic centers.

Language

French is the main official language, while Duala is widely spoken in the city.

Fun Fact

Douala is one of the most energetic and fast-growing cities in Central Africa.
